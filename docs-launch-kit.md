## Demo workflow

1. A customer submits a website form, sends an email, leaves a voicemail note, or requests information after hours.
2. The system identifies the lead type, urgency, service area, and missing information.
3. The AI drafts or sends a first response such as: “Thanks for reaching out. We can help. What time window works best for a quick call today?”
4. The owner receives a concise alert with customer contact info, service needed, urgency, and recommended next action.
5. The lead is logged in a spreadsheet or CRM.
6. A weekly report summarizes leads captured, response times, booked calls, unresolved follow-ups, and recommended workflow improvements.

## Implementation architecture

**Simple beta stack:**
- Website form/email/SMS trigger
- Automation router using Zapier, Make, n8n, or a custom webhook
- AI classification and response draft
- Google Sheet or CRM lead log
- Owner notification by email, SMS, or Telegram
- Weekly summary generated from lead log

**Human-control options:**
- Draft-only mode: AI drafts replies for human approval
- Guarded auto-reply mode: only approved response categories are sent automatically
- Full auto-reply mode: system sends predefined follow-up messages and escalates exceptions
