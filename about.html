About Us

Meet the team dedicated to transforming your business through technology

Our Story

InternetCharlotte.com was founded with a simple mission: to bring enterprise-level technology expertise to small and medium businesses that deserve the same quality solutions as the Fortune 500.

With over 30 years of experience in software engineering and architecture, our founding team recognized that many businesses struggle to find technology partners who truly understand their needs. Too often, companies are sold solutions that don't fit, or they're left with systems they can't maintain.

We believe technology should enable your business, not complicate it. That's why we start every engagement by understanding your business model, your challenges, and your goals. Only then do we design solutions that make a real difference.
